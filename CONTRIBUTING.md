# Contributing

eris is open source under the [Apache License, Version 2.0](LICENSE). You may
read, use, modify and redistribute it under that licence.

## We do not accept outside contributions

eris is developed by its maintainers. We do not accept pull requests, patches,
issues or feature requests from outside the project:

- Pull requests from anyone who is not a collaborator on this repository are
  blocked.
- Issues and discussions are turned off.

This is not a judgement of any contribution. It keeps the project's review
load, licensing and direction in one place.

## Forks are welcome

Fork eris and change it however you like, within the terms of the licence.
Keep the `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files with any redistribution, and mark the
files you changed.

## Support

Support is available to paying customers at
[support@sibylline.group](mailto:support@sibylline.group).

## Security

Never report a vulnerability in public. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
